## 2. Common Issues & Quick Fixes

### Issue 1: No HRW Activation Email
- **Cause:** Sent to application email address; might be in spam/junk; or account is queued in next onboarding batch.
- **Action:** Check spam/promotions folder. In the interim, **host your event on HRC (Community)** so your monthly schedule is not delayed. Escalate to Technical Lead (`Sreesanth`) if activation does not arrive.

### Issue 2: Contest Link Errors / Disappears from Dashboard
- **Cause:** Temporary platform anomaly or misconfigured privacy settings.
- **Action:** Retain exact contest URL. Verify test status from account dashboard. Report immediately to Program Manager (`Sanskruti`) if contest is live.

### Issue 3: Contest Closed Before Participants Finished
- **Cause:** Contest reached scheduled end timestamp.
- **Action:** **Closed contests cannot be reopened.** Always build in 15–30 minute buffer time.

### Issue 4: Winner Rewards Not Activated
- **Cause:** The submitted email address differed from the participant's registered HackerRank account email.
- **Action:** Confirm the participant's exact HackerRank profile email, then resubmit with that specific candidate's details to the Program Manager.

### Issue 5: College / University Not Listed in Dropdown
- **Cause:** New or unindexed institution.
- **Action:** Select **"Other"** and manually enter the official college name.

### Issue 6: Who Counts as a "Participant"?
- **Definition:** Any attendee who attempts and submits at least one question or code execution on the contest platform. Simple registration without submission does not count toward reward thresholds.

## 3. Communication Protocol & Escalation Directory

- **Program Manager (`Sanskruti`):**
  - Scope: Rewards activation, onboarding, welcome kits, merchandise shipping, HackerRank engineer speaker/judge requests, general program inquiries.
- **Technical Lead (`Sreesanth`):**
  - Scope: Platform bugs, HRW access, SkillUp integration, technical contest environment issues.
- **Design Lead (`Nitish`):**
  - Scope: Brand assets, official certificate templates, visual asset verification.

### Best Practices:
- Use public group channels for questions and tips that benefit all ambassadors.
- Send winner lists, reward spreadsheets, and individual escalations via Direct Message.
- Avoid duplicate follow-up pings; messages are processed sequentially.
- Never promise unconfirmed speakers, merchandise, or rewards to participants before official confirmation.

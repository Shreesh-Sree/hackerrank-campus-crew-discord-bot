# HackerRank Campus Crew — Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

## 1. Coding Contest SOP (21-Step Process)
1. **Define Objective:** Skill-building, competitive ranking, or campus-wide visibility.
2. **Select Format:** DSA, MCQ, debugging, or role-based assessment.
3. **Select Difficulty:** Tailor to participant background (beginner, intermediate, advanced).
4. **Prepare Questions:** Choose from question library or write custom questions in HRW/HRC.
5. **Configure Platform:** Set time limits, test-case scoring, allowed languages, and access settings.
6. **Test Questions:** Solve and execute every single question yourself before publishing.
7. **Create Contest:** Finalize contest metadata, title, and description.
8. **Set Duration:** Include buffer time for evaluations. Remember that contest end times are hard cutoffs.
9. **Verify Registration Link:** Test link in incognito mode to ensure seamless self-registration.
10. **Promote:** Follow the 14-day promotion timeline.
11. **Conduct Event:** Launch contest at the scheduled time.
12. **Live Monitoring:** Actively observe dashboard metrics, submissions, and anomalies.
13. **Real-time Resolution:** Address candidate environment or question ambiguities while contest is live.
14. **Finalize Rankings:** Extract leaderboard immediately upon contest conclusion.
15. **Verify Winners:** Verify identities and exact emails tied to their HackerRank accounts.
16. **Submit Winners:** Send winner list and contest URL to Program Manager (Sanskruti).
17. **Track Activation:** Confirm winners receive their Infinity Plan and platform credits.
18. **Issue Certificates:** Generate and distribute participation and winner certificates.
19. **Publish Results:** Announce winners publicly (LinkedIn, campus channels) with consent.
20. **Document Event:** Prepare a brief summary report for records.
21. **Post-Mortem Review:** Note feedback and actionable improvements for the next event.

## 2. Hackathon SOP
- **Design Decisions:**
  - **Theme & Tracks:** Focus areas (AI/ML, Web3, FinTech, EdTech, Open Innovation).
  - **Problem Statements:** Open-ended for creativity vs. specific for scoring consistency.
  - **Eligibility & Teams:** Solo, pairs, or teams up to 4.
  - **Tech Stack:** Allowed languages, frameworks, or open-ended.
- **Milestones:**
  - Registration deadline -> Hacking window -> Submission cutoff -> Judging & Demos -> Winner declaration.
- **Submissions:** GitHub repository URL, architecture/project write-up, demo video (2-3 min) or live pitch.
- **Judging Criteria:**
  1. Technical Execution (code quality, completeness, architecture)
  2. Creativity & Originality
  3. Problem Relevance & Practical Impact
  4. Presentation & Demo Quality
- **Recommended Hybrid Format:** Embed a 1–2 hour HackerRank algorithmic or coding contest segment within the hackathon to automate objective scoring.
- **External Judges:** Request HackerRank engineer judges via Program Manager well in advance.

## 3. Workshop & Bootcamp SOP
- **Duration:** 60 to 120 minutes per session (or multi-part series of 2-3 sessions).
- **Core Topics:** DSA fundamentals, full-stack stacks, AI/ML basics, technical interview prep.
- **Hands-On Element:** Every session should include live practice on a custom HackerRank challenge or test.
- **Speaker Selection:** Ambassador, technical club leads, alumni, or an in-house HackerRank engineer requested through the Program Manager.
- **Wrap-up:** Collect feedback form, distribute resource links, issue certificates, and post social recap.

## 4. Reward Tiers & Activation SOP
### Reward Tiers
| Participant Count | Rewards for Winners |
| :--- | :--- |
| **< 300 Participants** | • 1-Year HackerRank Infinity Plan<br>• Mock Interview Credits<br>• 6-Month Access to 1,500+ AI Tools |
| **≥ 300 Participants** | • All the above, PLUS **Official HackerRank Merchandise** (shipped to Ambassador as SPOC for distribution) |
| **All Participants** | Official Participation Certificate (for anyone who submitted code/answers) |

### Winner Activation Workflow
1. Leaderboard freeze & rank confirmation.
2. Collect **Full Name** and **exact email linked to winner's HackerRank account**. (Mismatched emails cause failed activations!).
3. Send email to Program Manager (`Sanskruti`) containing: Event Name, Contest Link, Total Participants, Ranked Winner Details.
4. Activation takes place directly on winner accounts (usually within 24-48 hours).
5. Follow up with winners to verify receipt in their HackerRank profile/email.
